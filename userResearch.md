Q1: what were your initial thoughts whenever you first stepped in front of the screen?
that my head was big, a little confused but i was like ok i think i know what to do - i've heard wave the wheat before but i've never actually done it. there wasn't a ton of guess work so not as stressful
i was like oh do i do something with my arms? do i use 1 arm or 2? do i put it a certain way? hearing the music validated that i was doing was right

Q2: how did you come across the thought to raise your hands and wave them?
i've attended one football game here, and i saw it on the screen say it at the game, and i've seen other people do it. it also showed ku students in the simulation

Q3: What were your likes about this project?
i really liked the music bc it felt like ku culture and tradition. i loved that the students were not smiling and sad until i put my arms up and they cheered. i also really liked that their arms matched my arms. it was also very sensative bc it knew whenever i only had one arm up vs both. and it was pretty instant

Q4: Dislikes?
i did something a few times that triggered the animation when i didn't mean to. like i was doing something random with my arm and it started the animation

Q5: If you could change one thing about this project, what would it be?
maybe make it more of a guessing thing? like when i saw wave the wheat, i kinda knew what to do. if someone who didn't go here saw this they wouldn't really know what to do.
