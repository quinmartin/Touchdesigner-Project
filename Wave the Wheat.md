CONCEPT
I am making a touchdesigner project for class that has to be interactive with the user. My plan is to make an interactive screen to teach potential new students at the University of Kansas the tradition of "waving the wheat", where they wave their arms side to side to symbolize a wheat field. The goal is to make it so that the user steps in front of the screen, and as they wave their hands side to side, a crowd of virtual people around them will also wave their hands in sync. For example: if the user waves their arms to the left, all the people on screen will also wave their arms to the left - same for to the right.
