## 10 Concepts

I am wanting to build a project based around sports - live broadcasts at games, media day shoots, etc. I want this to be interactive with the player or fans, and something I can hopefully sell to large teams or programs in the future

1. Live fan broadcast to be used during a game and displayed on a stadium or arena big screen. As a fan lifts their hands up to cheer, team-branded elements or particles will emit from outside their silhouette, creating a fun and interactive fan experience. There would most likely be a text prompt on the screen to help guide the fans into triggering the animation

2. An interactive media day background that tracks the player's motions to affect the display board background video. For example, if a player raises his arms to "get loud", the video board will change based on if his arms are up or down

3. Another interactive media day background, still tracking the player's motion. This time, though, there is a big text prompt on the video display background (ex. "JAYHAWKS") the player could do a Superman emote across the "Jayhawk" title on their jersey, and as their hands wipe across their chest, the text in the background reveals in the same way.

4. Another interactive media day background, still tracking the player's motion. This one also involves a big text prompt on the background. This time, as the player raises his/her arms, the text gets more and more energetic by shaking and bouncing around the screen (think AE 'wiggle' effect). As the player lowers their arms, the text gets calmer

5. Another interactive media day background, still tracking the player's motion. There will be clips of a team's hype video playing in the background, and based upon the movement of the player, different parts of the video will play

6. Another interactive media day background, still tracking the player's motion, but this time involving lasers. There will be multiple lasers set up around the player, and based on the player's movements, the lasers could do different things. For example, the lasers could trace around the player, dance around and go crazy, set a strobe, move slowly across the player, etc. Lots of possibilities, I'm just not sure if I have access to lasers (and not sure how I would connect the hardware to TD)

7. An in-stadium or welcome center video board display for the University of Florida Gators. Florida has a famous hand signal where they "chomp" their arms together in an up-and-down motion to signify an alligator. The video display could give different outputs based on if the user's hands are open or closed together. This would be a really fun way for new fans to learn about the traditions of the university

8. Same idea as 7, but for the Kansas City Chiefs' tomahawk chop

9. A "get loud" or "make some noise" overlaying motion graphic for a stadium or arena that ACTUALLY reacts to the noise of the fans. They always have like a "decibel meter" graphic that pretends to react to the audio level, but it rarely ever works. This could take the input of the decibel meter from the fans and get more or less busy, creating a fun interactive environment

10. A basketball minigame that allows the user to do a shooting motion to "shoot" a virtual basketball into a virtual hoop. It could give fun pop ups like "THREE!" or "Nice Shot!" if they 'make' the basket. TD would have to detect not only the shooting motion of the user, but also the aim, direction, and power that the user does
